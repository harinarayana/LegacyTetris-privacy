Privacy Policy for Legacy Tetris

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information and data are collected, used, and protected when you play Legacy Tetris ("the Game"). We are committed to prioritizing user privacy through minimal data collection and transparent practices.

1. Information We Collect

A. Local On-Device Data

Legacy Tetris operates primarily as an offline-first application. Your game progress, local high scores, selected visual themes, audio settings, and control configurations are stored locally on your device (via Android DataStore/SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to external servers operated by us.

B. Third-Party Analytics and Advertising Data

To provide free gameplay supported by advertisements, Legacy Tetris integrates third-party SDKs, specifically Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP).

These third-party services may automatically collect certain device parameters and non-personal diagnostic data, including:

  • IP address (for general geographical region detection)
  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID, IDFV, or Android ID)
  • Ad interaction events (views, clicks, impressions)
  • App performance and crash logs

2. How Data is Used

Data collected by integrated third-party services is used exclusively to:

  • Deliver contextual or personalized advertisements (Banner, Interstitial, and Rewarded Video Ads).
  • Manage ad frequency capping and prevent ad fraud.
  • Comply with privacy regulations (such as GDPR and CCPA) by managing user ad preferences and consent choices.

3. Third-Party Privacy Policies

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of the third-party providers integrated into Legacy Tetris:

  • Google Play Services & Google AdMob Privacy Policy

4. User Privacy Choices & Consent

Depending on your location, you may have specific privacy rights regarding personalized advertising:

  • European Economic Area (EEA) / UK (GDPR): Users in applicable regions are presented with a consent choice via Google UMP upon initial app startup to accept or customize personalized ad preferences. You can reset your privacy preferences at any time through the app settings or your Android device system settings.
  • California Residents (CCPA/CPRA): You have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for targeted advertising by managing your Android advertising ID settings.
  • Resetting Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your device Advertising ID at any time via Settings > Privacy > Ads on your Android device.

5. Children's Privacy

Legacy Tetris does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). If we become aware that personal data from a child has been collected through third-party ad networks without verified parental consent, we will take immediate steps to remediate the situation.

6. Data Security

All local game data remains securely stored within your application sandbox on your Android device. Third-party ad transactions are transmitted over secure HTTPS channels standard in modern mobile operating systems.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Effective Date" at the top.
